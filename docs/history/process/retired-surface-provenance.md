## Evidence Boundary

This provenance can prove that a surface was retired, where current owner refs live, and which no-resurrection rule applies. It cannot prove App release readiness, stable/latest promotion, domain readiness, family production readiness, runtime/provider truth, owner receipt authority, artifact authority, quality verdict, candidate adoption, or same-cohort release evidence.

When a retired surface becomes necessary again, reopen the relevant current owner contract, validator, test, workflow, source module, or active owner doc first. Do not restore it as compatibility, fallback, alias, facade, wrapper, duplicate test, prose oracle, or process-history shortcut.
